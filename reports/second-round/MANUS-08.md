## Executive summary

The hard reading gate was completed before application-source inspection. The review then expanded across relevant fetched worker branches and their completion/report evidence, not just `main`. The original 26-route × 4-viewport sweep covered `main`, MANUS-03, and the earlier MANUS-10 snapshot `00410475`: 312 route/viewport cases, all HTTP 200. After the final fetch advanced MANUS-10, its latest changed files were refreshed in the isolated preview and specifically retested at `8607868`.

The latest MANUS-10 candidate fixes the chat-close smooth-scroll restoration, `/apply/` stepper overflow, missing widget loaders on several routes, and the MANUS-03-only quiz syntax regression. I confirmed the chat fix independently: at 1440×900 and 390×844, and at 390×844 with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, the saved scroll position was restored immediately and remained unchanged after 800 ms. A separate stale-position edge case remains: a redundant unlock callback after the user has moved the page restores the previous saved Y on `main` and MANUS-10. MANUS-03's pre-decrement lock-count guard prevents that narrow case, but MANUS-02 and MANUS-10 both change the same shared chat file, and MANUS-03's integration build records genuine conflicts. The original close-scroll animation is fixed on the latest MANUS-10 candidate; do not conflate that resolved candidate issue with the duplicate-unlock edge case or with integration on `main`.

Other findings remain: the homepage/settings-widget architecture and position-sync contract are inconsistent across routes; the mobile panel extends below the viewport; the document root still computes `scroll-behavior: smooth` even under reduced-motion emulation; and design-system contrast and admin-theme decisions need their owners. No application patch was made because the confirmed defects touch concurrently owned shared files or require the documented product/design decision. This report-only boundary follows the MANUS-08 stop-at-ownership instruction.
