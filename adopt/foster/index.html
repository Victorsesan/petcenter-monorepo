Who Needs You

Who We Need Foster Homes For

Not every animal that comes through our doors is ready to be listed for adoption right away. Fostering bridges that gap.

Neonatal Kittens & Puppies

Bottle-fed litters too young for the shelter environment need round-the-clock care until they're weaned and old enough to be spayed/neutered.

Medical Recovery

Post-surgery pets, or animals recovering from injury or illness, heal faster in a quiet home than in a busy shelter — we handle the medical side, you handle the couch time.

Behavioral Rehab

Shy, fearful, or reactive animals often just need a calm one-on-one environment to decompress before they can be safely shown to adopters.

Overflow & Space Relief

When intake spikes, fosters free up shelter space for the next animal in need — sometimes for as little as one or two weeks.

Hospice & Senior Care

Older or terminally ill animals deserve a comfortable home for whatever time they have left, rather than living out that time in a shelter kennel.

Weekend & Event Fostering

Can't commit to weeks? Some pets just need a break from the shelter for a weekend — great for a first-time foster trial run.

Our End of the Deal

What Pet Center Provides

Fostering shouldn't cost you anything but time and a little space. Here's everything we cover.

  • Food, litter, and basic supplies for the full foster period.
  • All veterinary care, including emergency visits, at no cost to you.
  • A crate, bedding, and starter supplies if you don't already have them.
  • A dedicated foster support line for questions, day or night.
  • Training for medical or behavioral fosters (bottle-feeding, medication schedules, etc.).
  • First right of refusal to adopt your foster pet, if you fall for them.

Questions

Foster FAQ

Do I need a fenced yard or a specific type of home?

No — apartments work fine for most foster pets. Some medical or behavioral cases have specific space needs, which we'll discuss during matching.

Can I foster if I already have pets?

Usually yes. We ask about your current pets' temperament and vaccination status during the application to make sure it's a safe match.

What if I fall in love and can't give them back?

It happens more than you'd think — fosters get first right of refusal to adopt. We'll just ask you to let us know as early as possible.

What if I need to end the foster early?

Life happens — just call our foster support line and we'll arrange pickup or a transfer to another foster as quickly as we can.
