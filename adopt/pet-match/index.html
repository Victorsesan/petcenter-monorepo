A reference photo is entirely optional, and only worth adding if it genuinely helps us picture what you're describing — a written description is often enough on its own. If you do include one, please make sure it's an image you own or have the rights to use: a screenshot of a photo you found online, rather than a saved or downloaded copy, respects the original photographer's rights.
