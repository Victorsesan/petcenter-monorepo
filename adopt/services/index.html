How We Help

Our Services

Adoption doesn't stop at "yes." From flexible payment plans to nationwide delivery, fostering, and support long after you bring your new best friend home — here's everything Pet Center offers to make the journey easier.

Flexible Payments Shipping & Delivery Foster Program Health Guarantee Starter Kit Post-Adoption Support
Payments

Flexible Payment Plans (BNPL)

An adoption fee shouldn't stand between an animal and a loving home. That's why every listing at Pet Center supports flexible ways to pay — split across a schedule that fits your budget, not the other way around.

Choose Buy Now, Pay Later at checkout for a short-term installment plan with no long-term interest, or Pay-in-4 to split the adoption fee into four equal, interest-free payments over six weeks. Prefer to pay once your new companion has settled in? Pay on Delivery lets you complete the adoption first and pay upon arrival for local and eligible shipped adoptions.

  • Available on every listing — the exact plans offered for a specific pet are shown right on its detail page.
  • No hidden fees — the payment schedule you agree to at checkout is the schedule you keep.
  • Track every upcoming installment from your Adoption Status Dashboard.
Browse Available Pets
Payment plan availability and exact terms vary by listing and are confirmed during your application — see a pet's detail page for what's offered for that specific adoption.
Getting Them Home

Shipping & Local Delivery

Not every perfect match lives around the corner. We work with pet-safe, climate-controlled ground and air transport partners so distance is never the reason a pet doesn't find its family.

Local to Beverly Hills? Choose hand delivery right to your door within our local service radius, with a Pet Center team member walking you through everything on arrival. Adopting from farther away? Nationwide shipping is available on eligible listings, fully insured and tracked door-to-door, with your pet's comfort and safety as the top priority the whole way.

  • Climate-controlled, pet-safe transport — never cargo hold, never unattended.
  • Shipping insurance included on every eligible shipped adoption — see your Status Dashboard for coverage details.
  • Delivery windows and costs are shown per listing before you apply — no surprise charges at the door.

Open Your Home

Foster Program & Listing Your Own Pet

Not ready for a full adoption, or need to rehome a pet of your own? Our growing self-service portal is where both journeys start.

Foster a pet temporarily while we find its forever family — we cover food, supplies, and vet care during the foster period, and a Foster-to-Adopt option lets you turn a trial stay into a permanent adoption whenever you're ready. If instead you have a pet of your own that needs a new home, our rehoming portal lets you create a listing so Pet Center can help match them with the right family, safely and at no listing fee.

  • Supplies and vet care included for the duration of any foster placement.
  • Foster-to-Adopt: fall in love during the foster period, finalize the adoption whenever you're ready.
  • No listing fee for rehoming a pet through Pet Center — safe, verified matching, our way.

Health & Peace of Mind

30-Day Health Guarantee & Pet Insurance

Every pet leaves Pet Center vaccinated, spayed or neutered where age-appropriate, and vet-checked — and our guarantee keeps you covered for the month that follows.

If a vet uncovers a pre-existing condition within 30 days of adoption, we'll help cover treatment or work with you on next steps — you're never left figuring it out alone. Want ongoing coverage after that? We've partnered with a leading pet insurance provider so accident and illness coverage can be activated right from your Adoption Status Dashboard, with a limited-time free trial period for newly adopted pets.

  • Every adoption includes a full medical history and vet-confirmed Health Record.
  • 30-day guarantee covers pre-existing conditions found by your own vet after adoption.
  • Optional ongoing pet insurance, with a free introductory period for new adopters.
Read the Full Guarantee
First Day Home

Adoption Starter Kit

The first 24 hours with a new pet shouldn't be spent making an emergency supply run. A Starter Kit can ride along with your adoption so your new companion walks into a home that's already ready for them.

Each kit is sized to the animal — a crate or carrier, a leash and collar or harness, a starter bag of food matched to their current diet, bedding, and a few age-appropriate toys. Add a Starter Kit during checkout on any eligible listing, and specify which items you'd like on the application — you tell us what your home still needs, we bring the rest.

  • Sized and stocked for the specific pet you're adopting — no generic one-size kit.
  • Food matched to whatever your pet was already eating in our care, to avoid a stressful diet switch.
  • Pick and choose items on the application — only pay for what you actually need.

Beyond Adoption Day

Post-Adoption Support & Training Resources

Adopting is the beginning of the relationship, not the end of ours. Our team stays reachable long after your pet comes home, and we connect adopters with real training and behavior support.

Have a question about settling in, introducing an existing pet, or a behavior that's new to you? Reach our support team through the floating contact button on any page. We also maintain a growing library of settling-in guides and species-specific care articles on our blog, and can point new adopters toward trusted local trainers and behaviorists in the Beverly Hills area for anything that needs hands-on help.

  • Direct support access for as long as you have your pet, not just during the application.
  • Settling-in and species-care guides available any time on our Blog.
  • Referrals to trusted local trainers and behaviorists whenever you need hands-on help.
