Lost & Found Pets

A place to report a missing pet or a stray you've found — separate from our adoption listings, since these animals already have (or are looking for) a home.

Reporting a lost pet

There's no automatic listing form yet — for now, get your pet in front of our team and community as fast as possible:

  • Contact our team with your pet's name, description, last-seen location and date, and a recent photo.
  • We'll help post it here on the board and check it against anything reported found.
  • Keep checking the "Recently Found" column below in the meantime.
Contact Our Team

Reporting a found pet

Thank you for helping a displaced pet get home. Here's how to list what you found:

  • Contact our team with where and when you found the animal, a description, and a photo if you have one.
  • We'll post it here and check it against current lost-pet reports.
  • If the animal needs immediate shelter, let us know — we can advise on safe short-term care.
