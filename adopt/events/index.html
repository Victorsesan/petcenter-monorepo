Types of Events We Run

While You're Here

Did You Know?

Fun Fact

Pets meet more adopters in the first hour of an event than in a typical week of regular walk-in visits — first impressions really do move fast.

Good to Know

Every pet at an event is already vaccinated, microchipped, and spayed/neutered — no extra vet trip required before you take them home.

Handy Tip

Bring a leash and carrier if you already have one at home — it's not required, but it makes a same-day adoption a lot smoother.

Before You Go

What to Expect at an Adoption Day

1

Walk in, no RSVP needed

Just show up during the listed hours — there's no sign-up required to browse and meet pets.

2

Meet as many pets as you'd like

Our staff and volunteers are on hand to introduce you and answer questions about temperament and care.

3

Found a match? Start the application on the spot

We can help you begin your adoption application right there at the event, on a tablet or your own phone.

4

Same-day adoptions are common

If everything checks out, plenty of visitors leave the event with their new pet the same day.

Questions

Events FAQ

Can I bring my current dog to meet a potential new pet?

Yes, at most events — leashed, well-socialized dogs are welcome. Off-site pop-ups may vary depending on the venue, so check the listing for that event.

Do I need to bring anything?

Just a valid photo ID if you're hoping to adopt same-day. Everything else — paperwork, carriers for small pets — we can provide on-site.

What if it's cancelled due to weather?

Off-site outdoor pop-ups occasionally get rescheduled for weather — we'll note that on the event listing as early as possible.

Can I host an event with you at my business or venue?

We're always open to new community partners — reach out through our contact page and we'll follow up.

Reach out and ask to be added to our events list — we'll let you know before the next one near you.
