Questions

Sponsorship FAQ

What happens if my sponsored pet is adopted?

We'll email you right away. Any remaining balance on a monthly plan rolls to another pet in need, or you can choose to stop there.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Sponsorship donations are processed the same way as other Pet Center donations — a receipt is provided for your records.

Can I sponsor as a gift for someone else?

Yes — choose the One-Time Gift option and add a dedication message; we'll make sure it's noted.
