Who We Work With

Our Partner Network

Every animal that comes through Pet Center touches at least one of these partners along the way.

Veterinary Clinics

Exams, vaccinations, spay/neuter, and emergency care for every pet before and after placement.

Rescue Groups

Partner rescues who transfer animals to us when we have space and resources to help.

Trainers & Behaviorists

Referral partners for adopters and fosters working through a behavioral challenge.

Transport Partners

Safe, humane transport for animals moving between shelters, clinics, and events.

A Few We Work With

Featured Partners

Veterinary
Beverly Hills Animal Hospital

Our primary veterinary partner for exams, vaccinations, and spay/neuter surgery.

Rescue
Westside Small Animal Rescue

Partner rescue focused on small mammals and exotics who transfer overflow animals to us.

Training
Pawsitive Steps Dog Training

Our go-to referral for adopters working through leash reactivity or basic obedience.

Transport
SafePaws Transport Co.

Handles humane transport for animals coming in from partner rescues across Southern California.

Local Business
Golden Triangle Pet Supply

Discounted starter supplies for every Pet Center adopter, right in the same shopping center.

Reptile & Aquatic
Beverly Hills Reptile & Aquatics

Specialist support for our reptile and aquarium categories — husbandry advice and equipment.
