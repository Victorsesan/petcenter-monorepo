How we build this estimate

A worldwide pet-shipping calculator only means something if the numbers behind it are real. Here's what actually drives the price.

Live pets & live animals

For dogs, cats, birds, reptiles, small mammals, and other live animals, we price against USDA Animal Welfare Act–compliant, IATA Live Animal Regulations–certified transport — the same standard used across the professional pet relocation industry. Costs are driven by distance from Los Angeles, weight, crate size (dimensional weight), and destination country.

  • Domestic ground transport typically runs $120–$900 depending on distance and crate size
  • Domestic cargo air typically starts around $450, up to roughly $1,200 for a large pet crossing the country
  • International relocation typically runs $650–$4,500+, door-to-door, depending on destination
Passenger-airline cargo holds for pets have narrowed sharply industry-wide, with most major carriers now limiting checked pets to military and government families and rules changing with little notice — which is exactly why our Cargo Air and Same-Day options route through IATA-approved freight and relocation partners rather than a standard airline checked-baggage counter.

Pet accessories, food & supplies

Crates, carriers, leashes, beds, toys, food, treats, and medication ship as standard parcels — no live-animal handling required — so we also price these against everyday USPS, UPS, and FedEx ground and international services, the same carriers most shipping calculators are built around.

  • USPS and UPS/FedEx Ground for most domestic accessory and food shipments
  • UPS and FedEx international services for accessories shipped outside the USA

Booking & deposit

Reserving a shipping slot requires a 50% deposit upfront, covering the handler slot, fuel, route coordination, crate inspection, vet documentation, and pre-departure health check. Your booking is confirmed once the deposit is received and verified.

Vet documentation

Every live-animal shipment needs a health certificate from a USDA-accredited veterinarian, dated within 10 days of departure, plus an IATA-compliant crate sized to the pet. We coordinate airline-specific paperwork and destination-state or destination-country entry requirements on your behalf.

Transit insurance

Standard coverage (up to $500, accident and handler liability) is included in every shipping fee at no extra cost. Premium insurance — emergency vet care, weather-delay protection, and up to 100% of pet value — is an optional add-on, typically 1–3% of the pet's declared value.

Returns on accessories

If you cancel an accessory or supply order before it ships, it's refunded in full minus any applicable fees. Once an item ships, customers typically cover return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect — in which case Pet Center covers it.
