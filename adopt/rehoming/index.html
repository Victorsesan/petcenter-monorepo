FAQ

How does verification work?

Merchants submit identity and organization information for review. A verified state is shown only after the review queue approves it.

How are badges earned?

Badges use verified status, activity, completed placements, and response-rate records. Public displays use the platform's reduced badge treatment.

How does reporting work?

A visitor can report a listing or conversation for safety review. Reports enter a separate admin queue and do not silently remove a listing.
