We're Here to Help

Can't Keep Your Pet?

If you're considering giving up a pet, you're not alone — and surrender isn't always the only option. Take a look at what might help before making a final decision.

Before You Decide

Most of the reasons pets are surrendered — behavior, cost, housing changes — have a resource behind them. A little support now can mean not having to say goodbye.

Temporary Fostering

Ask a trusted friend or family member to foster your pet short-term while you work through a housing move, a medical situation, or a financial rough patch — often all it takes to avoid a permanent surrender.

Low-Cost Training & Behavior Help

Behavior issues are one of the most common, most fixable reasons pets are given up. Ask us for a referral to a low-cost trainer or behaviorist before assuming the situation can't improve.

Financial-Assistance Resources

If cost is the barrier — food, vet care, pet deposits — ask us about current community financial-assistance programs and partner resources before deciding surrender is the only path.

If Surrender Is Still the Right Choice

We understand it isn't always avoidable. Here's what to expect if you do need to bring your pet to us.

1

Reach out first

Contact our team before arriving — intake is by appointment so we can make sure space and staff are ready for your pet.

2

Share your pet's history

Vaccination records, behavior notes, diet, and medical history help us place your pet faster and more safely.

3

Intake & evaluation

Your pet is examined, given any needed care, and evaluated so we can match them with the right future home.

4

Listed for adoption

Once ready, your pet is published to our adoption listings, the same process every Pet Center pet goes through.

Talk To Our Team

Whether you want to explore an alternative or need to start the intake conversation, tell us a bit about your situation and we'll follow up.
