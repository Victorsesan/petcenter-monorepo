Open Roles

Ways to Volunteer

Pick whatever matches your time, skills, and comfort level around animals. Most volunteers rotate between a couple of roles once they've settled in.

Adoption Events

Greet visitors, walk them through the adoption process, and help match people with the right pet at our weekend adoption days and off-site pop-ups.

Weekends, 3–4 hr shifts

Dog Walking & Enrichment

Walk, play with, and socialize dogs and cats between visits — the single biggest factor in how quickly an anxious animal settles in and becomes adoptable.

Flexible, any day

Admin & Front Desk

Answer phones, manage intake paperwork, and keep our pet records tidy. A great fit if you'd rather help from behind the scenes than out on the floor.

Weekday mornings

Photography & Social Media

A good photo can cut a pet's time-to-adoption dramatically. Bring your camera (or your phone) to shoot adoptable pets or help run our social accounts.

Once or twice a month

Transport

Drive pets to vet appointments, between our facility and partner clinics, or to and from off-site adoption events. A reliable vehicle and a calm hand help.

As-needed, short notice

Fundraising Events

Help plan and staff our seasonal fundraisers — set-up, check-in tables, auction support, or just extra hands wherever the event needs them that day.

Seasonal
Getting Started

What It Takes to Volunteer

Most roles are open to anyone willing to learn — here's what we ask before your first shift.

  • Minimum age 16 (14–15 welcome with a parent or guardian volunteering alongside them).
  • A one-time orientation session (about 45 minutes) covering safety and basic animal handling.
  • A minimum commitment of one shift per month, so animals see familiar, consistent faces.
  • Closed-toe shoes for any animal-handling role (dog walking, transport, adoption events).
  • No prior experience needed — we train on the job for every role above.

Questions

Volunteer FAQ

Do I need to bring anything to orientation?

Just a photo ID and closed-toe shoes if you're signing up for a hands-on role. We provide everything else, including any handling equipment.

Can I volunteer if I'm allergic to certain animals?

Absolutely — admin, photography, fundraising, and transport roles all involve minimal or no direct animal contact if that's a better fit.

Is there a background check?

For roles that involve working alone with animals off-site (like transport), yes — a basic check is part of onboarding. Other roles don't require one.

Can volunteer hours count toward school or court-ordered community service?

Yes — we sign hour-verification forms for school programs, and separately for court-ordered service on a case-by-case basis. Ask at orientation.
