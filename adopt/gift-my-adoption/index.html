For Adopters

Gift My Adoption

Adopting soon? Create a shareable page so friends and family can chip in on your adoption fee, starter kit, or shipping — like a registry, just for your pet.

Not the same as Sponsor a Pet. Sponsor a Pet lets anyone support any pet still waiting for a home. Gift My Adoption is for you, the adopter, once you have a pending application — it's tied to your own adoption, not open to the public to browse.

How It Works

1

Look up your application

2

4

Bring your pet home

Contributions apply directly to your adoption — nothing is held or redirected elsewhere.

Questions

Gift My Adoption FAQ

What if my application isn't approved?

If your application doesn't go through, we'll reach out about redirecting any contributions — either refunded to your contributors or donated to Pet Center, your choice.

Do contributors need a Pet Center account?

No — anyone with your link can contribute without creating an account.

Can I use this for a pet I already adopted?

Gift My Adoption is built around a pending application's adoption code, so it's meant to be set up before or right at adoption — not after the fact.
