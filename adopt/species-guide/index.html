Questions

Readiness FAQ

These numbers seem general — will my specific pet be different?

Yes, always. These are starting ranges by species, not guarantees. Breed, age, health, and individual temperament all shift the real numbers — ask us about specifics for any pet you're interested in.

What if I'm not ready for the species I wanted?

That's a useful thing to know now. Consider fostering that species first to test the fit, or look at a lower-commitment species while your situation changes.

Does this affect my adoption application?

No — this guide is just for you, nothing here is submitted or tracked against your application. It's purely a self-check before you apply.
